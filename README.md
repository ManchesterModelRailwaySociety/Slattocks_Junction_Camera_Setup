Slattocks Junction Camera Setup

You will need the following items…
 
The Camera	The USB Cable	The PC	The Power Supply
			

    • Secure the camera to the lighting rig using vinyl tape or masking tape
    • Run the camera output lead along the lighting strut and secure
    • Plug the camera output cable into the USB extension cable
    • Place the PC on the table in a secure place near to the Signalman’s position
    • Run the USB extension cable along the lighting strut and down to the PC
    • Plug the USB extension cable into the PC
    • Plug the power unit into the PC and a mains socket (the PC will need to be 
	constantly powered as the battery does not hold any significant charge)
	The power plug at the PC end should have a blue glow round it. 
    • Open the PC and press the power-up button in the right-hand hinge
    • Allow the PC to go through its startup routine – The PC uses Linux Mint 
	instead of Microsoft Windows. You will find it very similar to Windows. In a 
	while you will see the Linux Mint Desktop.
    • The program to run is called Cheese (as in “say 'cheese' to the camera”) 
	Double click the Cheese icon on the PC screen. A window will appear showing 
	an image from the internal PC camera. To view the image from the WebCam click 
	on the icon to the right of the Burst button. 
    • To display the image full screen click on the “hamburger” icon (three 
	parallel horizontal lines and then on the Fullscreen option. 

To shutdown the PC move the cursor to the Linux Mint button at the bottom left of the 
screen then on the red power button. Finally click on the red Shutdown button that appears 
in the centre of the screen,
